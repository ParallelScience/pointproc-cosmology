By implementing the suggested changes—specifically moving to a hierarchical Bayesian forward model and incorporating a selection function for the radial profile—the paper will shift from a "negative result" (the model is broken) to a "methodological contribution" (how to correctly infer HOD parameters from discrete, binned cosmological data). This significantly increases the "Publishability Delta" by providing a robust framework for future researchers, satisfying the criteria for structural robustness and reviewer confidence.
